# FlatBook Privacy Policy

**App:** FlatBook (com.digitalcraftapps.flatbook)  
**Developer:** Digital Craft Apps  
**Contact:** digitalcraftapps@gmail.com  
**Effective date:** 7 November 2026

FlatBook is an Android app that helps apartment owners and residents track monthly maintenance, expenses and payments. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have. By using FlatBook you agree to this policy.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information.** When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address and a unique account ID from your Google account. We never see your Google password.

**Apartment data you enter.** FlatBook stores what you add to an apartment: the apartment name, blocks, flat numbers, resident or flat names, maintenance amounts, payment status, expenses (with their optional date, block or flats), meter readings, major maintenance items, backups and notes. If you are the owner, your name and email are stored with the apartment. Please enter only information you are entitled to share with the people who use the apartment.

**Shared admins.** If you give another person admin access, their Gmail address is stored so that the app can recognise them.

**Audit log.** For apartment owners, the app keeps a record of who changed what and when, including the email of the person who made each change. Entries older than about six months are removed.

**Premium plan and purchases.** Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We receive only the details needed to unlock Premium: the product bought, the order ID, the purchase time and the expiry date. We never receive or store your card or bank details. Your plan (free, free trial or Premium) and its expiry date are stored in your Premium record, on your user record, and on the apartments you own.

**Free trial.** If you start the 3-month free trial, we record your email address, the account ID and the trial end date so that each email address can use the free trial only once. No payment information is involved.

**Notifications.** If payment notifications are on, the app uses Firebase Cloud Messaging, which creates a device token and subscribes your device to a topic for each apartment you open. This lets us send you a message when a payment is recorded.

**Device count.** Each apartment keeps a simple number showing how many phones have added it. It does not identify devices or people.

**Information stored on your device.** The app saves your settings, the list of apartments you added, your default apartment and notification choices on your phone.

**Files you choose.** If you export an apartment, the app saves one encrypted file to your Downloads folder. It contains the apartment's data, shared-admin emails and backups, together with your email address and plan at the time of export. Only the FlatBook app can read this file, and keeping it safe is your responsibility. If you import an apartment, the app reads only the file you pick. If you export a report, the app saves it to your Downloads folder. When you share an apartment's invite picture or report picture, the app creates the picture on your phone and hands it to the app you choose; you decide who receives it.

**Technical information.** Like any app that uses the internet, FlatBook's service providers (Google and Firebase) may receive technical data such as your IP address and device type when the app connects. The app also loads its fonts from Google Fonts.

**What we do not collect.** FlatBook does not collect your location, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data. It has no advertising and does not use an advertising ID. It does not include third-party analytics or crash-reporting tools.

## 2. How we use information

We use the information to:

- sign you in and keep your account secure;
- store and show apartment, maintenance and expense records to the people you share them with;
- send payment notifications you have enabled;
- unlock and manage Premium, and allow one free trial per email address;
- create and restore backups, and export and import apartments;
- respond to support requests and fix problems;
- meet legal obligations and prevent fraud or misuse.

We do not sell your information and we do not use it for advertising.

## 3. Who can see your apartment data

Anyone who has an apartment's share code can open that apartment in the app and view its records, without signing in. Treat the share code like a password and share it only with the residents who should see the records. Only the owner and shared admins can change data (a shared admin may be limited to certain blocks or flats), and only the owner can see the audit log, backups and the device count.

## 4. Who we share information with

We share information only with the service providers needed to run the app:

- **Google Firebase and Google Cloud** (sign-in, database, cloud functions, notifications), which store and process app data on our behalf.
- **Google Play** (billing and purchases).
- **Google Fonts** (font delivery).

These providers have their own privacy policies and may process data on servers outside your country. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the app.

## 5. How long we keep information

We keep your account and apartment data until you delete it or delete your account. Apartment audit logs keep about six months of history, and the app keeps only the latest backups. When you delete your account, your records are removed as described below. Two small records are kept without any link to your account: the free-trial record (your email address and the trial end date) so the same email cannot start another free trial, and any purchase token so that a deleted purchase cannot be claimed again. Routine copies in provider backups or logs can take a short time to expire.

## 6. Deleting your account and data

You can delete your account at any time inside the app: **Account → Delete my account**. This permanently deletes:

- your account and profile;
- every apartment you own, with its maintenance, expenses, flats, backups and audit log;
- your Premium record in FlatBook, including your plan, expiry date and order IDs;
- your shared-admin access in other people's apartments.

The free-trial record and purchase tokens described in section 5 are kept, unlinked from your account.

Deleting your account does **not** cancel a Google Play subscription. If you have an annual plan, cancel it in Google Play (Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions) so you are not charged again. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its policies.

If you cannot use the app, email digitalcraftapps@gmail.com from the address linked to your account and ask us to delete your data. We will act on the request within a reasonable time. Files you exported to your phone are not deleted by this; delete them from your Downloads folder yourself.

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can view and correct your apartment data in the app, turn payment notifications on or off, remove an apartment from your list, export your data, and delete your account. Depending on where you live, you may also have rights under data-protection laws, including India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, to access, correct or erase your personal data. To use these rights, contact us at digitalcraftapps@gmail.com.

## 8. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections and protected by Firebase security rules and sign-in checks. Exported apartment files are encrypted. No method of storing or sending data is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please keep your Google account, exported files and share codes safe.

## 9. Children

FlatBook is intended for adults who manage or live in apartments. It is not directed at children under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

## 10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date above. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app or on the Google Play listing. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.
